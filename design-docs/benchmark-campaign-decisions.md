# Benchmark Campaigns: Decision Log

This log records the decisions behind [benchmark-campaign.md](./benchmark-campaign.md),
the reasons for them, and the facts they depend on. Update it when a decision
changes. Marwen Abid made D1 to D20 on 2026-09-29 and 2026-09-30. After the
spec review and a requirements review, he confirmed D1, D4, D5, D15, D16, D17
and D20 on 2026-09-30. He amended D6 and D12 and added D21 to D27. He
confirmed D28 to D33 on 2026-09-30. On the same day, the architect of the fix
pass added D34 to D37. The architect also amended D3, D11, D12, D16, D19,
D21, D22, D25, D28, D31, D32 and D33. The Status column gives the date of
each amendment.

Status values:

- **Confirmed**: Marwen or the architect made or approved the decision.
- **Proposed**: the spec contains it, but nobody confirmed it. Confirm it
  before you build on it.
